FAQs

Why do I have to pay to book a call?

You are paying to skip the queue of people who were never serious. This fee will be fully adjusted against your programme fee when you join. It literally only costs money if you never intended to take action.

What happens on the call?

No fluff. We audit your current habits and show you exactly how we would fix your routine. We are committed to give you the absolute bang for your buck.

What happens after the call?

You can enroll in our personalised 1:1 coaching programme. Towards the end of the call, we shall offer our programme details, our systems and how our coaching process can help you with your goals.

Who is this programme for?

People over 30, busy professionals, leaders, and entrepreneurs who are ready to invest in a coaching process for their long-term health. It is also for you if you have metabolic conditions (e.g., high BP, cholesterol, fatty liver, prediabetes, diabetes, PCOS).

Is this a pitch for a generic workout plan?

Absolutely not. If you want a cookie-cutter "30-day shred," download a free app. This call is to build a custom, habit-based strategy that actually fits YOUR lifestyle.
